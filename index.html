A Special Journey

Happy Birthday, My Love

Stage 01 • The Beginning

The Train

We met on a train for the very first time on 28th April 2024—a date I will hold close to my heart for the rest of my life. It was undeniably the luckiest day of my life, the day the absolute best thing happened to me. As my station grew closer and time was running out, I quickly made an Instagram account right there just so I could ask for yours. I will never forget the look on your face and the way you looked back at me in that moment. That ordinary train ride became the best journey of my entire life.
Stage 02 • Our Safe Haven

Banaras

Banaras holds a place so deep in our hearts because it’s where we always find our way back to each other. That sacred city gave us the peaceful space to truly know one another. From our movie dates to quiet late-night walks along the Banaras ghats, every single moment felt like magic. I still smile thinking about how you reached down to hold my hand, with mine resting safely in yours—a constant reminder of how much shorter I am than you! Banaras witnessed every single one of our "firsts," and it will forever carry the warmth of our love.
Stage 03 • My Heart & Soul

Admiring You

I will always choose to admire you. I admire the dedication and passion you put into your work, and the boundless love and care you show toward your family. You have been my greatest teacher and my biggest strength. I will never forget how patiently you teach me everything, pushing me to learn and constantly motivating me to become better. You’ve shaped the way I see the world. I know deep in my heart that I could never, ever love anyone else the way I love you.
Yours, Shivangi
